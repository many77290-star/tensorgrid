The fine print

What exactly you're buying

Refresh, not real-time

TensorGrid refreshes every 15 min (Pro: SLA-guaranteed within 30 min of upstream publish). For tick-level data, we'll tell you honestly: we are not the right tool.

Not investment advice

You're buying normalized data and a terminal, not signals, not picks, not recommendations. The 'beats TAO' flag is descriptive, not prescriptive.

No token custody, no keys

TensorGrid does not hold TAO, alpha tokens, or API keys. Nothing touches your custody. Public tier requires no auth.
